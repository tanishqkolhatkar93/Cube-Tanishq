Business problem

Teams lose money when each ecommerce decision is checked in a different place.

Receiving evidence, FBA prep, MFN packing, return condition and recovery reports often live apart. Operators must manually connect them, which slows decisions and hides reimbursement opportunities.

PhotosPrepReturnsFeesClaim

User

Warehouse or marketplace operations associate who needs a clear next action for one unit.

  • Reviews photos and documents
  • Checks FBA or MFN route
  • Approves or reviews final decision

Customer

Amazon seller, ecommerce brand or fulfillment team trying to reduce loss and manual effort.

  • Wants faster unit handling
  • Needs proof for claims
  • Needs fewer missed reimbursements

Before CUBE

Manual, scattered and hard to audit

  • Operator checks photos in one place and fees in another.
  • FBA and MFN rules are easy to mix up.
  • Recovery claims are missed because evidence is not connected.
  • Unclear decisions become tribal knowledge.

After CUBE

One workflow with evidence behind every decision

  • One unit moves through the right ecommerce path.
  • Every agent records a verdict, reason and source references.
  • Recovery sees upstream evidence and flags claimable fees.
  • Human override is available and audited.

How We Solve It

One unit enters the console. The orchestrator chooses the right path and each manager leaves traceable evidence.

01

Route

FBA units go through Prep. MFN units go through Pack. Returned units also go through Returns.

02

Decide

Each agent makes a bounded decision for its domain and records verdict, reason, confidence and refs.

03

Recover

Recovery compares fees against upstream evidence and recommends claims when charges are contradicted.

04

Audit

Operators can inspect evidence or override a verdict without deleting the original record.

ReceivingIs the inbound unit acceptable?

Agent Roles In Ecommerce

Each agent has one job, so the system is easier to trust and explain.

01

Receiving

Confirms inbound unit and carton evidence before operations begin.

Example: carton count, SKU match, visible damage.
02

Prep

Checks FBA labels, barcode coverage and handling compliance.

Example: FNSKU label, barcode covered, handling marks.
03

Pack

Checks MFN seller-fulfilled pack and ship readiness.

Example: order contents, packaging, shipment photo.
04

Returns

Grades returned product state and resale readiness.

Example: identity, completeness, condition.
05

Recovery

Finds claimable fees using evidence from earlier stages.

Example: inbound defect fee contradicted by prep PASS.

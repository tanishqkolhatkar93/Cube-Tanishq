Business problem

Amazon sellers lose time and money when FBA, MFN, returns and recovery decisions live in separate tools.

User Operations associate reviewing units, photos, labels, returns and reimbursement reports.
Customer Ecommerce seller or warehouse team that needs fast, auditable decisions.
Pain Manual handoffs, unclear evidence, missed claims, duplicate checks and hidden uncertainty.

How we solve it

  • 1

    Route the unit. FBA goes through Prep, MFN goes through Pack, returns go to Returns, and fees go to Recovery.

  • 2

    Make every agent accountable. Each manager produces its own evidence record with verdict, reason, refs and hashes.

  • 3

    Show the business outcome first. Operators see clean, review, blocked or claim recommended before opening details.

  • 4

    Keep humans in control. Overrides are audited; original evidence remains intact.

01

Receiving

Checks inbound cartons, unit identity and visible damage before downstream work starts.

02

Prep

Handles FBA readiness: labels, barcode coverage, handling requirements and compliance.

03

Pack

Handles MFN seller-fulfilled packaging and shipment readiness when the route is not FBA.

04

Returns

Grades returned items and decides whether the unit can be resold or needs review.

05

Recovery

Compares fees against upstream evidence and flags claimable Amazon reimbursements.
